About Belpak Packaging

What began in 1981 as a small family initiative in Hathras, Uttar Pradesh, has become a trusted name in packaging across India. Belpak Packaging is run by the third generation of the same family, led by Abhay Goyal, a chemical engineer.

We make handcrafted Meena boxes and jar hampers, gift hamper and bakery boxes, dry fruit and mithai packaging, and — through our corrugation unit, Maa Vaishno Corrugators — corrugated, printed, offset and mono carton boxes.

What we make

  • Meena boxes & luxury gift boxes
  • Gift hamper boxes
  • Dry fruit boxes and mithai boxes
  • Corrugated boxes, printed boxes, mono cartons and pharmaceutical boxes

Our journey

  • 1981 — founded in Hathras
  • 1991 — expanded into decorative packaging
  • 2001 — customised packaging and corporate gifting
  • 2011 — luxury Meena box collections
  • 2016 — network across India
  • 2026 — continuing the legacy with innovation
