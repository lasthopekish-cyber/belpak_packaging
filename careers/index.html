Careers at Belpak Packaging

Belpak Packaging is a third-generation packaging manufacturer in Hathras, Uttar Pradesh, making gift hamper boxes, Meena boxes, dry fruit boxes and corrugated packaging since 1981. We're growing and hiring for the roles below. All roles need a minimum of 2–3 years' experience; salary is as per experience.

Sales Manager

Location: Hathras, Uttar Pradesh · Type: Full-time · Experience: minimum 2–3 years · Salary: as per experience

Grow Belpak's B2B business with sweet shops, hamper makers, wedding planners, corporate gifting teams and brands.

What you'll do

  • Find and win new B2B accounts across Delhi NCR, Uttar Pradesh and other markets
  • Manage existing clients, repeat orders and festive-season demand
  • Prepare quotations with the production team and follow up to closure
  • Meet monthly sales targets and report on the pipeline

What we're looking for

  • Minimum 2–3 years in B2B sales, ideally packaging, printing, gifting or FMCG
  • Strong negotiation and follow-up skills
  • Comfortable travelling to meet clients
  • Good spoken Hindi and working English

Tele Caller

Location: Hathras, Uttar Pradesh · Type: Full-time · Experience: minimum 2–3 years · Salary: as per experience

Be the first voice our customers hear — call leads, share catalogues and turn enquiries into orders.

What you'll do

  • Call inbound and Instagram / WhatsApp leads within the day
  • Share catalogues, prices and MOQs, and qualify each requirement
  • Log every call, follow-up date and outcome in the CRM
  • Hand over confirmed orders to the sales and production team

What we're looking for

  • Minimum 2–3 years in tele-calling, inside sales or customer support
  • Clear, polite spoken Hindi; basic English
  • Comfortable with WhatsApp Business and a CRM or Google Sheets
  • Target-driven and organised

Product Designer

Location: Hathras, Uttar Pradesh · Type: Full-time · Experience: minimum 2–3 years · Salary: as per experience

Design the boxes, hampers and prints that make Belpak packaging stand out.

What you'll do

  • Create box artwork, prints and patterns for new collections and custom client orders
  • Prepare print-ready files and dielines with the production team
  • Make mock-ups and catalogue visuals
  • Adapt client logos and branding to packaging

What we're looking for

  • Minimum 2–3 years in packaging, print or product graphic design
  • Strong in CorelDRAW, Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop
  • Understanding of dielines, print colours and finishes like foil and emboss
  • A portfolio of packaging or print work

Product Developer

Location: Hathras, Uttar Pradesh · Type: Full-time · Experience: minimum 2–3 years · Salary: as per experience

Turn new packaging ideas into products we can make well, at the right cost.

What you'll do

  • Develop new box structures, inserts and hamper formats
  • Select boards, papers and materials and work out costing
  • Make and test samples with the factory team before production
  • Work with vendors on materials, finishes and timelines

What we're looking for

  • Minimum 2–3 years in packaging, rigid box, corrugation or product development
  • Knowledge of kappa board, duplex, corrugated and rigid box construction
  • Hands-on with sampling and costing
  • Practical, detail-oriented problem solver
