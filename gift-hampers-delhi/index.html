Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver gift hamper boxes to Delhi?

Yes. Belpak Packaging delivers hamper boxes, Meena boxes and dry fruit boxes to Delhi from our factory in Hathras, about 3½ hours by road. Delivery charges are as per actuals and confirmed with your order.

What is the minimum order for hamper boxes in Delhi?

Most gift hamper and bakery boxes start at 50 pieces; a few premium pieces like the Designer Bag with 2 Potli and the Fancy Suitcase start at 10. Meena boxes and dry fruit boxes are quoted for your quantity.

Can I get customised hamper boxes with my logo in Delhi?

Yes. Share your size, quantity and artwork and the Belpak team confirms printing, finish and price for your order before production.

How much do hamper boxes cost?

Catalogue prices start from Rs. 10 per piece for small treat boxes, about Rs. 45–105 for most hamper boxes, and Rs. 130–840 for handcrafted Meena boxes and jar hampers. GST and delivery are extra.
